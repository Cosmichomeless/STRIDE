## English (en)

**Subtitle** (23): Run tracker, no account

**Keywords** (75): `running,run,gps,pace,tracker,distance,route,jogging,workout,privacy,offline`

**Description**

STRIDE records your runs with GPS: route, distance, time and pace, live on a map. No account, no ads, no tracking: your runs stay on your iPhone.

- Live route map, pace and distance
- Keeps recording with the screen off
- Pause and resume; recovers if the app closes
- History of every run with its route
- Kilometers or miles, run goals, light and dark mode
